# AI Reference Prompts

Reference site:

https://site-structure-architect-v2-users-d.vercel.app

Use this site as a structural and marketing reference for new design versions of the landing page.

## Core Rule

The reference is not a strict visual template.

Preserve:
- the order of the large sections;
- the purpose of each section;
- the approximate height and scale of the large sections;
- the marketing flow;
- the topbar;
- the fullscreen Hero;
- the split quiz;
- projects, tariffs, audience, and FAQ as separate major sections.

You may change:
- visual style;
- fonts;
- colors;
- images;
- card styling;
- micro-animations;
- details inside cards;
- decorative elements;
- mood and visual language.

Do not change:
- the order of the large blocks;
- the meaning of the blocks;
- the number of key sections;
- the main selling logic.

The site is a landing page for a digital specialist who creates selling websites, landing pages, service websites, portfolios, SaaS interfaces, and complex projects. The goal is to explain value, show the process, build trust, and lead the visitor to a request.

## Base Structure

1. Hero: fullscreen first viewport with topbar, large headline, short description, CTA, and a visual area.
2. Problems: three reasons why a site does not bring requests.
3. Comparison: business-card website vs selling website.
4. Process: six stages of work.
5. Quiz: split layout with intro, progress, and first question.
6. Projects: six project cards.
7. Tariffs: three tariff cards.
8. Audience: six audience cards.
9. FAQ + final CTA + footer.
